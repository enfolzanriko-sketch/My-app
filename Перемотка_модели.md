## 📨 Сообщение 1 — описание (отправить первым)
```
Hi! I'm making a Roblox game called "Rewind: Midnight Hotel". Please help me create concept art. First read the description, then wait for my requests.

THE GAME: a kid-friendly mystery game for 6–10 players in an old hotel. Some players are secret "Painters" who trap other guests inside paintings: a golden frame grows around the guest and they become a painting with their own avatar on it. A detective can "rewind" and see what a player did 3 seconds ago. Painters travel through old portraits on the walls like secret passages. Players use blocky Roblox-style avatars.

ART STYLE (use it for every image):
- stylized low-poly 3D look, toy-like, chunky rounded shapes, big simple forms, no tiny details;
- cozy but mysterious old hotel: dark wood, red velvet, golden picture frames, teal-green wallpaper, warm lamp light, dark corridors with soft shadows;
- slightly spooky but friendly and funny, like an animated cartoon — no blood, no gore, no scary faces;
- easy to build as low-poly 3D models in Blender.

Reply "Ready" and wait for my first request.
```

- **Самые важные картинки:** №2 (холл), №4 (портреты) и №5 (заточение) — по ним видно весь стиль игры.
